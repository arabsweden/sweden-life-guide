## Data Privacy & Confidentiality Principles

1. **Sensitive Data Redaction (Personnummer & Health)**:
   - Automated detection for Swedish Personal Identity Numbers (`YYYYMMDD-XXXX`).
   - Warning modal prompting users to confirm masked text before submission.

2. **Attachment Retention Policy**:
   - Uploaded attachments (images, PDFs, DOCX, audio) are held in encrypted temporary storage purely for processing.
   - Automatically purged after answer generation unless explicit user retention consent is provided.

3. **Consent Management**:
   - Separate consent records for saving query history, dispatching emails, and anonymous analytics.
   - No user data is submitted for external model training without explicit consent.

4. **User Rights**:
   - Single-click option to download personal stored history or purge saved data.
