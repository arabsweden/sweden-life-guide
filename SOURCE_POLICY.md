## Official Source Accreditation Policy

This platform operates strictly on grounded official sources from accredited Swedish government domains (`.se`).

### Allowed Official Domains Whitelist

1. `migrationsverket.se` (Swedish Migration Agency)
2. `skatteverket.se` (Swedish Tax Agency & Population Register)
3. `forsakringskassan.se` (Social Insurance Agency)
4. `arbetsformedlingen.se` (Public Employment Service)
5. `socialstyrelsen.se` (National Board of Health and Welfare)
6. `1177.se` (National Healthcare Guide)
7. `csn.se` (Swedish National Board of Student Aid)
8. `skolverket.se` (National Agency for Education)
9. `transportstyrelsen.se` (Transport Agency & Driving Licenses)
10. `bolagsverket.se` & `verksamt.se` (Companies Registration & Business)
11. `imy.se` (Privacy Protection Authority)
12. Official Swedish Municipalities (`*.se`)

### Citation & Review Rules

- Social media posts, forums, and unverified blogs are strictly excluded as primary legal sources.
- Every grounded answer must explicitly link to an accredited domain URL and specify the last review date.
- Swedish original text takes precedence in case of translation discrepancies.
